Concept / Explainer

Why This System Was Built

Barangay Kalubihan still reports emergencies the manual way — walk-ins, phone calls, radio messages. Details get lost, response is delayed, duplicate calls go unnoticed, and no record exists of who reported what or how fast it was handled.

This system exists to change that: one web platform connecting residents, dispatchers, department admins, responders, and administrators — a single digital pipeline from incident report to verified resolution, where every second and every record counts.

Chapter 1

Objectives of the Study

  1. Develop a web-based system that enables residents to file emergency reports with photo evidence, incident details, and automatic GPS location pinning, and to track the status of their reports.
  2. Implement a dispatcher command center that allows real-time triage of incoming reports, filing of incidents on behalf of callers, and assignment of departments and responders.
  3. Provide role-based portals for department admins and responders to act on dispatched incidents, mark arrival on scene, and resolve cases with proof documentation.
  4. Integrate real-time notifications and an announcement feed to keep residents and responders informed during emergencies.
  5. Establish resident ID verification and role-based access control to ensure that only authorized users can access and modify system functions and records.
  6. Develop reporting and analytics tools that provide operational metrics and exportable incident records for accountability and data-driven decision-making.
Chapter 1

Scope and Limitations

Scope

  • Web-based system for barangay staff — login, incident management, department & responder management, resident verification & access control, announcements & reports
  • Mobile-responsive resident interface — register with valid ID, file reports with photo evidence and GPS, track report status, view announcements & contacts
  • Admin web panel — user & role management, resident review, departments, metrics & report generation
  • Geofence-enforced service area — resident reports verified against the Kalubihan boundary
  • Duplicate-incident clustering — corroborating reports grouped for one dispatch

Limitations

  • Requires a stable internet connection (web-based, cloud-hosted)
  • Serves Barangay Kalubihan only — no multi-barangay or municipal coordination
  • No SMS alerting or offline reporting — in-app realtime + email only
  • No voice calls — coordination through the incident workflow
  • Location accuracy depends on device GPS and browser permissions

Conclusions

Conclusions

  • The system replaces manual, fragmented barangay reporting with a single digital pipeline — report → triage → dispatch → resolution — with every step tracked and auditable.
  • Role-based portals deliver each user exactly the tools they need: residents report with photo + GPS evidence, dispatchers triage on a live map, responders act and document resolution, admins govern access.
  • Integrity is enforced at the database, not just the UI: ID verification gates reporting, photo evidence is required, geofencing blocks out-of-area reports, and duplicate incidents are clustered automatically.
  • Realtime notifications and the announcement feed close the information gap between the barangay and its residents during emergencies.
Recommendations

Recommendations

  • SMS alerting and offline mode — reach residents without data connectivity; queue reports for sync when offline.
  • Multi-barangay scaling — extend the boundary model and role hierarchy for municipal-level coordination.
  • Integration with hotlines/IoT — direct linkage with 911-style dispatch feeds and barangay sensors (CCTV, flood monitors).
