WBS Tasklist

The Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) for the Kalubihan Emergency Response System outlines the key phases and tasks needed to plan, develop, and implement the project successfully.

It begins with the Initiation phase, where the project’s purpose is defined through project identification, requirement elicitation, and stakeholder meetings.

In the Planning phase, the team conducts a literature review, designs the system architecture, plans the network topology, and assesses potential risks to ensure a solid foundation.

The Execution phase includes the technical development of the system—database setup, frontend/backend coding, and module testing to ensure functionality.

During the Control phase, functional testing, user validation, and bug fixing ensure the system meets quality standards and user needs.

Finally, the Closure phase involves deploying the system, preparing documentation for future reference, and presenting the final output to stakeholders. This structured WBS ensures the project follows a clear, organized, and goal-oriented workflow.
